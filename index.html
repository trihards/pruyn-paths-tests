Write down

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings, General, About), Wi-Fi or cellular, and whether Low Power Mode is on.
  • For each test: did it load, did it run, and what broke. Screenshots help; include the time bar at the top of the screen.

Then test the link itself

Text this page’s address to a friend and post it in Slack. Does a preview picture appear? Does tapping it open Safari, or a viewer inside Messages or Slack? Do the tests behave the same way there?

What these pages are

Everything here is synthetic: the trail is made up and the photos are generated images. The pages do not collect or send anything; they only fetch public map pictures from the U.S. Geological Survey (with Esri as a backup). This site is generated from the private Pruyn Paths project and is not edited by hand.
